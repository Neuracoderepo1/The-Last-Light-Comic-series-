# The Last Light — Story Bible

## Premise
Nova City has lived under Lumen, a planetary illumination network, for eighty-three years — built, publicly, to protect humanity from whatever followed the Long Blackout. Mara Vale, a Lumen signal technician, has started hearing things the network insists aren't there.

## Locked Canon
These facts are established as of Episode 01 and must not be contradicted in later episodes:

- **02:17 A.M.** is the canonical timestamp of first contact with The Signal.
- The blackout in Episode 01 is the first Lumen shutdown in **83 years**.
- The Signal's first words are **"HELLO, MARA."** — it knows her name unprompted.
