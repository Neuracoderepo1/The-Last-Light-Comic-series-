# Art Direction — The Last Light

## Palette (locked — do not deviate without updating `styles.css` tokens)
- Near-black `#07090d`, midnight `#0d1420`, graphite `#171b22`
- Muted silver `#8a92a3`, cold white `#f4f6fb`, pale blue `#c7d3ea`, moonlight `#dfe6f5`
- Accent (used sparingly): electric blue `#4d8dff`

## Tone
Cinematic, restrained, premium. No neon-cyberpunk cliché. Cold blues and near-black dominate; warmth/organic tone is reserved and meaningful (see Episode 01, panel 8 — the first real night sky in 83 years).

## Panel Art Pipeline
1. Placeholder: procedural abstract SVG, generated by `panelArtSVG()` in `app.js`, keyed by each panel's `art` string (e.g. `skyline`, `console`, `blackout`).
2. Final asset: produce/commission at `/assets/panels/S{season}E{episode}-P{panel-number}.webp` (e.g. `S01E01-P03.webp`).
3. Swap: replace the panel's `.panel-art` inner SVG with an `<img src="…" alt="…">` pointing at the final asset. Keep the `panel-art` wrapper's `aspect-ratio:16/10` so layout doesn't shift.
4. Keep art **abstract/non-representational** until final asset is ready — no stock or placeholder clip-art.

## Naming & Continuity
- File naming: `S{season}E{episode}-P{panel}.webp`, zero-padded (`S01E01-P09.webp`).
- Any new character, location, or object introduced in art must be logged in `/universe/story-bible.md` under that episode's canon changes before the episode is marked `published`.
