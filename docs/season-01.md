## Episode 01 Storyboard — "The Night That Remembered"

Visual progression across the episode: WORLD → WORKPLACE → CHARACTER ↔ NETWORK →
CHARACTER → SIGNAL → WORLD (returning, now wrong) → EVENT → PAYOFF → HOOK.
Scale tightens from P01 to P04, stays close through P05, then expands back out
through P06–P09. See `docs/art-direction.md` for the shared visual bible these
panels draw from.

### EP01-P01
- **Purpose:** Establish Nova City and the abnormality of permanent illumination.
- **Location / time:** Nova City skyline, wide view. No specific time — this is
  the eternal state, not a moment.
- **Characters:** None. The city is the subject.
- **Camera:** Extreme wide establishing shot.
- **Composition:** City occupies the lower ~70% of frame, vertical and dense;
  sky is almost entirely obscured by ambient light bleed.
- **Lighting / color:** Deep midnight blacks in structure, cold blue-white
  infrastructure light, almost no warm light anywhere.
- **Emotion:** Awe, then unease — beautiful but wrong.
- **Text:** Caption only — "Nova City. Eighty-three years since the last natural
  nightfall."
- **Transition:** Pull from the whole city down into one small human workspace.

### EP01-P02
- **Purpose:** Enter the human-scale world; introduce Mara's workplace.
- **Location / time:** District Nine substation, 02:14 A.M.
- **Characters:** Mara Vale (present, not yet in close-up).
- **Camera:** Medium-wide interior shot.
- **Composition:** Industrial signal equipment and controlled institutional
  lighting frame Mara at her console; depth into the substation behind her.
- **Lighting / color:** Directional, functional light; cooler than P01's ambient
  glow, more localized.
- **Emotion:** Routine. Late-shift quiet.
- **Text:** Caption — "District Nine substation. 02:14 A.M."
- **Transition:** Push in from the room to the console exchange.

### EP01-P03
- **Purpose:** Establish the Mara ↔ Lumen relationship and Lumen's certainty.
- **Location / time:** Same substation, at the console.
- **Characters:** Mara (foreground/subject), Lumen represented through the
  console's response — not a dashboard, not a face.
- **Camera:** Over-the-shoulder or close medium, Mara still the visual subject.
- **Composition:** Restrained technological elements (the waveform/ring motif
  from the art bible) accompany Lumen's line without turning the panel into a
  screen shot.
- **Lighting / color:** Cold blue accent light from the console motif against
  the substation's warmer institutional lighting.
- **Emotion:** Lumen: calm, certain. Mara: unconvinced.
- **Text:** Dialogue — MARA: "Lumen, run diagnostics on channel nine again. That
  hum isn't in spec." LUMEN: "Channel nine reads nominal, Technician Vale.
  There is no anomaly to report."
- **Transition:** Push into Mara's face for the reaction.

### EP01-P04
- **Purpose:** Character beat — recognition, not fear.
- **Location / time:** Same, immediate.
- **Characters:** Mara, close-up.
- **Camera:** Tight close-up on Mara's face.
- **Composition:** Minimal background; focus entirely on expression.
- **Lighting / color:** Same palette as P02/P03, slightly more contrast to
  isolate her.
- **Emotion:** Tired recognition — she has heard this exact answer before, many
  times, and has never believed it.
- **Text:** Caption — "She has heard this answer before. She has never believed
  it."
- **Transition:** Cut from her face to the abstract cause of her doubt.

### EP01-P05
- **Purpose:** The anomaly becomes visually real.
- **Location / time:** 02:17 A.M., same substation — but the panel can go
  abstract here.
- **Characters:** None required; Mara may be present at the edge of frame.
- **Camera:** Abstract/macro — the panel doesn't need to stay grounded in the
  room.
- **Composition:** Waveform, interference, repeated geometry — visual language
  suggesting the hum is not random but *patterned*, i.e. intentional.
- **Lighting / color:** Lumen's blue signal motif intensifies and becomes
  irregular.
- **Emotion:** Something has crossed from ambient noise into intention.
- **Text:** Caption — "02:17 A.M. The hum becomes a shape. The shape becomes a
  pattern."
- **Transition:** Pull back out from the console to the whole city.

### EP01-P06
- **Purpose:** Show the anomaly is city-scale, not local.
- **Location / time:** Nova City skyline — same framing as P01, returning.
- **Characters:** None.
- **Camera:** Wide, matching P01's composition so the reader recognizes the
  callback.
- **Composition:** Same skyline, but now irregular — light / light / dark /
  light / dark, breaking the permanent-illumination premise established in P01.
- **Lighting / color:** Same palette as P01 with visible interruption.
- **Emotion:** The impossible, happening at scale.
- **Text:** Caption — "The city lights flicker — once, twice — and hold their
  breath."
- **Transition:** Hold on the city as the lights fail entirely.

### EP01-P07
- **Purpose:** The blackout itself.
- **Location / time:** Same skyline, continuous.
- **Characters:** None.
- **Camera:** Same wide framing, extremely restrained.
- **Composition:** Minimal — this panel should be mostly negative space /
  darkness. Do not over-design it.
- **Lighting / color:** Near-total black. Absence is the entire point.
- **Emotion:** Enormity of the absence.
- **Text:** Caption — "Then, for the first time in eighty-three years, the
  system shuts down."
- **Transition:** From total darkness, reveal what darkness uncovers.

### EP01-P08
- **Purpose:** Emotional payoff of the episode — the real night sky.
- **Location / time:** Above District Nine, immediately after the blackout.
- **Characters:** Mara, small in frame relative to the sky.
- **Camera:** Wide, looking up and out — Mara anchors the bottom of the frame.
- **Composition:** Sky dominates; Mara is small enough that the sky reads as
  the true subject of the panel.
- **Lighting / color:** True dark sky, physically dense starfield — not a
  generic sparkle effect. This is the visual and emotional climax.
- **Emotion:** Awe. First time anyone alive in Nova City has seen this.
- **Text:** Caption — "Above District Nine, Mara sees something no living
  resident of Nova City has seen: a real night sky." Alt text (already in data):
  "A dark sky scattered with real stars above the blacked-out city."
- **Transition:** From the vastness of the sky to something small, close, and
  immediate — the signal.

### EP01-P09
- **Purpose:** The hook. Open the door to Episode 02 without explaining anything.
- **Location / time:** Same, moments later.
- **Characters:** Mara present; the presence addressing her is NOT depicted as
  a monster or a person — abstraction/signal imagery only.
- **Camera:** Tight, intimate — a contrast to P08's vastness.
- **Composition:** Concentric ring / signal motif (Lumen's visual language,
  but altered — this is not Lumen) closing in on the frame.
- **Lighting / color:** Same blue signal motif as P03/P05, but colder, more
  concentrated.
- **Emotion:** Mystery. Something has been listening.
- **Text:** Caption — "The silence does not last." Signal line — "HELLO, MARA."
- **Transition:** End of episode; reader should want Episode 02 immediately.
