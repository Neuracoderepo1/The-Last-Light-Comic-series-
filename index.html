The Premise

A city that never learned to fear the dark — until it had to.

Nova City has lived under Lumen, a planetary illumination network, for eighty-three years. Publicly, Lumen exists to protect humanity from whatever followed the Long Blackout. Mara Vale maintains it. She has also started hearing things it insists aren’t there.
